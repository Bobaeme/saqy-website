Our Services

Drone Crop Spraying

Precise application of fertilisers, pesticides or growth regulators via agricultural drone.

$50 / hour Indicative rate
  • Pre-flight field assessment included
  • Targeted, uniform spray coverage
  • Faster than manual spraying teams
  • Coverage area log & spray report
  • Suitable for paddy fields & plantations

Aerial Field Monitoring

Drone aerial surveys delivering imagery and vegetation maps to reveal what ground inspection misses.

$20 / lot Indicative rate
  • High-resolution aerial RGB imagery
  • NDVI / vegetation index mapping
  • Field boundary & area measurement
  • Annotated PDF field report
  • Identifies waterlogging & stress zones
ℹ️ Lot definition and size must be confirmed before pricing is finalised. Please contact us to discuss your field dimensions.
⚠️ Subject to Brunei DCA regulatory approval before operations commence.
