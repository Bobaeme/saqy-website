The Problems

Challenges We Aim to Address

Many farming decisions in the region are made without reliable field data. We're working to change that.

Limited field visibility at scale

Walking a large field takes hours and still misses problem areas. Aerial surveys cover the same ground in minutes.

Inefficient water and irrigation management

Without maps showing wet or dry zones, water is applied uniformly — wasting resources and missing stressed areas.

Decisions made without data

Input scheduling and harvest timing often rely on experience alone. Field data supports faster, more confident decisions.

Uneven spray coverage

Manual spraying teams produce inconsistent results. Drone spraying improves coverage consistency and reduces drift.

High cost of traditional surveying

Satellite imagery can be expensive or low-resolution for small plots. Drone surveys offer a more accessible middle ground.

Technology that doesn't fit local needs

Off-the-shelf precision ag tools are often built for large-scale Western farms. We're designing for Brunei's context.
