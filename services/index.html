Our Services

Saqy Fly provides two targeted agricultural drone services designed for practical application in paddy and plantation farming.

💧 Available for Pilot / Booking

Crop Spraying Service

$50 / hour Indicative rate

Our drone crop spraying service delivers precise, uniform distribution of approved fertilisers, crop protection treatments, and biological inputs directly over your fields. By utilising aerial application, spraying can proceed even when fields are muddy, reducing soil compaction and physical crop damage.

Service Details & Scope

Pre-Flight Planning

Assessment of field layout, obstacle clearance, flight path boundary setup, and weather window checks.

Precision Drone Application

Even droplet dispersal and uniform canopy penetration, significantly reducing chemical waste and operator exposure.

Post-Operation Summary

Flight log verification, treated area confirmation, and application volume summary for farm records.

Operational Availability Notice: Availability depends on equipment readiness, certified drone operators, suitable field and wind conditions, and all required regulatory approvals from the Brunei Department of Civil Aviation.

Aerial Field Monitoring

$20 / lot Indicative rate

Systematic aerial surveys providing high-resolution RGB imagery and multispectral vegetation mapping. Aerial surveys uncover variations across large acreage that cannot easily be detected from the ground, such as patchiness, waterlogging, or uneven crop establishment.

Service Details & Scope

High-Resolution Aerial Orthomosaic

Stitched detailed aerial imagery of your entire plot for visual inspection and boundary mapping.

Vegetation & Crop Health Map

NDVI and spectral indices highlighting areas of vigor, weed emergence, or crop stress.

Summary Report

Annotated PDF report outlining key zones of observation to support on-the-ground management decisions.

Lot Definition & Pricing Terms: The exact definition and acreage of a "lot" must be confirmed in consultation with the farm owner before finalizing pricing. Survey operations remain subject to Brunei DCA regulations.
