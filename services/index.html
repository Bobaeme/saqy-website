Available

Drone Crop Spraying

Agricultural drones equipped with spray tanks apply fertilisers, pesticides, herbicides, or growth regulators across your field with controlled, even coverage — far faster than a manual team and with less drift than ground machinery.

This service is suited to paddy fields, oil palm, fruit orchards, and other plantation crops where ground access is difficult or where chemical use needs to be optimised.

What's Included

Pre-flight field assessment
Spray plan preparation
Drone spray operation
Coverage map report
Application volume log
Post-spray briefing
Not included: Supply of chemicals or fertilisers. Customer must provide approved agrochemicals. We do not advise on fertiliser type or rates — consult a qualified agronomist.
⚠️ All drone spraying operations are subject to Brunei DCA approval and applicable pesticide regulations. We will confirm regulatory status before confirming bookings.

Aerial Field Monitoring

Our drones fly systematic grid patterns over your field, capturing high-resolution RGB and multispectral imagery. The data is processed into maps that reveal what ground-level inspection misses: waterlogged zones, uneven growth, pest patches, and irrigation coverage gaps.

Output is delivered as a practical report and, where applicable, GIS-compatible files for further analysis.

What's Included

Pre-flight field walkthrough
Systematic aerial survey flight
High-resolution RGB imagery
NDVI / vegetation index map
Field area measurement
Annotated PDF report
Important: NDVI maps identify zones of vegetation stress or difference — they do not diagnose specific nutrient deficiencies or diseases. Results should be reviewed alongside agronomist assessment and, where needed, laboratory soil or tissue analysis.
⚠️ Drone survey operations are subject to Brunei DCA approval. We will confirm operational clearance before scheduling flights.

Data Analytics & Advisory

We are developing tools that combine drone data with seasonal records to provide advisory insights on irrigation scheduling, input timing, and crop health trends. This service is experimental and not yet commercially available.

We are actively looking for pilot farms and research partners to co-develop this capability. If you are interested in being an early collaborator, please contact us. Any AI-assisted analysis is experimental and should not replace professional agronomist advice or laboratory testing.
